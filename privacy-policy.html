Privacy Policy for Bonobo Games

Effective Date: 20-07-2026

Bonobo Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our games on mobile (Android and iOS) and PC (Steam), including any associated in-app purchases (IAPs), advertisements, online game services, and third-party services.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not collect, store, or process personal data directly from users of our games ourselves.

However, our games integrate software development kits (SDKs) and platform services from third parties, such as:

  • Ad mediation and networks (LevelPlay by ironSource, Unity Ads, Google AdMob)
  • Analytics providers (GameAnalytics)
  • App stores and platforms (Google Play, Apple App Store, Steam)
  • Online game services (Google Play Games Services, Apple Game Center)

These third parties may collect data automatically as part of providing their services.

2. Information Third Parties May Collect

Third-party SDKs and services integrated into our games may collect and process the following types of data:

  • Identifiers: Device ID (IDFA, GAID), IP address, and platform account/player IDs
  • Usage Data: App usage events, sessions, ad views, clicks, and interactions
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, screen resolution, language, battery level
  • Crash Data: Diagnostic and performance data
  • Game Progress Data: Achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves handled via Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center
  • In-App Purchase Information: Skins or other purchases processed through Google Play, the App Store, or Steam
  • Location (approximate): Based on IP address

This data is used for purposes such as analytics, personalized advertising, fraud prevention, and app functionality.

3. Advertising and Tracking

Our mobile games may show advertisements served through LevelPlay mediation (a Unity / ironSource platform). Through this mediation, ads may be served by the following networks: Google (AdMob and Google Bidding), Unity Ads, Mintegral, ironSource Exchange, and Digital Turbine (DT Exchange). These ad networks may track your device using advertising identifiers (e.g., IDFA or GAID) and may use cookies or similar technologies to serve interest-based ads.

On iOS, we comply with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You will be asked for permission to allow tracking when required.

4. Analytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand how our games are played (e.g., sessions, progression, and gameplay events). This helps us improve game design and performance. GameAnalytics may process device identifiers and usage data as described in its privacy policy.

5. Online Game Services

Our games may use Google Play Games Services (Android), Apple Game Center (iOS), and Steam (PC) to provide features such as achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves. When you use these features, the respective platform processes your player/account ID and related game data under its own privacy policy.

6. In-App Purchases (IAPs)

Purchases of virtual items, such as skins, are processed securely through Google Play, the Apple App Store, or Steam. We do not have access to or store your payment details.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal data to us, please contact us, and we will delete the information.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain privacy rights under local laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA). To exercise your rights, please refer to the privacy controls offered by your device, app store, platform, or the third-party providers we integrate with.

Examples:

  • Reset your device's advertising identifier
  • Limit ad tracking in your device settings
  • Revoke tracking permission on iOS (Settings > Privacy > Tracking)
  • Manage your Google Play Games, Game Center, or Steam account/privacy settings

You can also request deletion of ad-related data via our Data Deletion Request page.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but since we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, data security is primarily managed by the third parties referenced above.

10. Third-Party Services

Please review the privacy policies of these third-party providers to understand how they handle your data:

  • LevelPlay / ironSource / Unity Ads Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob / Google Bidding Privacy Policy
  • Mintegral Privacy Policy
  • Digital Turbine (DT Exchange) Privacy Policy
  • GameAnalytics Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Apple Privacy Policy (App Store & Game Center)
  • Steam (Valve) Privacy Policy
